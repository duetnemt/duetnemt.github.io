Connected software for non-emergency medical transportation providers.

Visit duetinc.com

Duet NEMT helps NEMT providers run scheduling, dispatch, drivers and billing from one connected system. Instead of retyping the same trip into a spreadsheet, a dispatch board and a billing tool, every trip lives as one record from booking to paid claim.

What Duet NEMT covers

  • Trip intake & scheduling – one-time and recurring trips, including dialysis standing orders.
  • Dispatch board – see every driver, vehicle and trip status in real time.
  • Driver mobile app – navigation, status updates, signatures and photos.
  • Billing – claims built from actual trip data, including CMS-1500 support.
  • Reporting – on-time performance, no-shows, utilization and revenue.

Why providers choose a connected NEMT workflow

Most late pickups, billing denials and dispatcher overtime come from the same root cause: trip data entered more than once. When the record created at booking is the same record the driver completes and the same record that becomes the claim, errors drop and payments arrive faster.
